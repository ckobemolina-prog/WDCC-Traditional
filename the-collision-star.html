About The Collision Star

A trusted partner after an accident

The Collision Star is the number one Dealer Preferred Collision Repair Center in the Greater New York Area. The company has built its reputation exclusively on dealer referrals and has been in business for three decades.

We will tow you in, get you a loaner, and manage 100% of your claim. Our skilled technicians assess the damage, order the necessary parts, and repair your vehicle to factory specifications with the finest quality parts available.

Before you call your insurer, call The Collision Star. Our team works to get customers everything they are entitled to under their insurance coverage and get them back behind the wheel faster.
