FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about repairs, insurance, rentals, and estimates.

How long will my repair take?

Repair times depend on the extent of the damage. The shop strives to work efficiently without compromising quality.

Do you work with all insurance companies?

The shop says it works with all major insurers to help simplify claims. Coverage and approvals depend on your policy and claim.

Can I get a rental car during repairs?

Onsite rental cars are available. Contact the shop to confirm availability, vehicle options, and rates.

What should I do immediately after an accident?

Contact the shop for help with next steps, including towing and insurance claim coordination.

Do you offer free estimates?

The shop offers free, no-obligation estimates in person and online.
