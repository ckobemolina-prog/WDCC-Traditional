GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before you get moving.

Can I arrange a replacement car during repairs?

Replacement transportation may be possible depending on provider inventory, eligibility, rental terms, and insurance approval. Contact WDC to discuss your next step, then confirm the arrangement with the rental provider.

Will my insurance pay for a rental?

That depends on your policy, claim circumstances, coverage limits, and insurer approval. Your insurer must confirm what is covered, for how long, and any amount you would pay yourself.

Can I book a specific vehicle on this website?

No. This page provides information and an inquiry path. It does not show live inventory or accept reservations. Vehicle availability, pricing, and terms must be confirmed directly with the provider.

What should I have ready when I contact WDC?

Have your preferred dates, a safe callback number, general vehicle needs, and repair questions ready.
